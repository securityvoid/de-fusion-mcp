  item: Size bound
  severity: blocking
  location: docs/specs/inspect/design.md:2285 (also docs/specs/inspect/requirements.md REQ-INS-017, docs/specs/inspect/tasks.md TASK-INS-009)
  scenario: given a revolve or extrude whose profiles hold 750 `line` curves, or 1500 `circle` curves, or one 1500-point spline, the system does count "points" only for sampled curves (the budget never says what a line, arc or circle costs), then serialises full-precision floats (about 18 characters per coordinate): 750 lines is about 70 KB and 1500 circles about 80 KB, and one 1500-point spline is about 58 KB before the other keys; `object_info` then fails `RESULT_TOO_LARGE`, which REQ-INS-017 says never happens from loops; it should have a cost per curve that is defined for every curve type, a budget whose worst case with full-precision floats stays under 65,536 bytes, and a test that proves it
  traces_to: req:REQ-CORE-023
  search: `MAX_TOTAL_POINTS`, `1500`, `profiles_truncated`, `under 64 KiB` in docs/specs/inspect
  fix: Define the budget as a cost per curve (line 2, arc 3, circle 2, sampled curve = its point count) and round output coordinates to 6 decimals, then lower `MAX_TOTAL_POINTS` until the worst case (all arcs, all splines) is under 60 KB at full-precision floats.
  verify: `pytest -q tests/test_inspect.py -k "object_info or profile"` (TASK-INS-009), through `actions.call("inspect","object_info",...)`: fakes with random full-precision floats at the cap for each of 20 profiles of 100 lines, 100 arcs, 100 circles and 100 splines; each serialised `result` is under 65,536 bytes with `profiles_truncated` true. The test fails if line, arc or circle curves are not charged against the budget.
